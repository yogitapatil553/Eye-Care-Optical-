Professional Optical & Clinical Services

From 14-step automated Japanese eye exams to 3D progressive fitting and emergency frame repairs—all under one roof.

14-Point Computerized Eye Check-Up

Complete refractive assessment using automated keratometer, slit-lamp biomicroscopy, and digital LED acuity charts to measure sphere, cylinder and axis down to 0.12 Diopters.

15 - 20 Mins Complimentary with Eyewear

Face Shape & Styling Assistance

Our experienced optical consultants analyze your cheekbones, bridge width, skin undertone, and wardrobe preferences to recommend shapes that highlight your finest features.

15 Mins Always Free

Progressive Lens 3D Digital Measurement

We digitally measure your monocular pupillary distance (PD), corridor height, and frame wrap angle. Eliminates peripheral swim distortion and ensures immediate adaptation.

20 Mins 30-Day Guarantee

Contact Lens Fitting & First-Time Trial

Gentle guidance for beginners. Includes corneal base-curve measurement, hands-on insertion/removal practice, hygienic care instructions, and trial diagnostic lenses.

25 - 30 Mins Trial Pairs Included

Prescription Lens Replacement

Love your current spectacle frame? Don't spend on a new frame! Bring it to our workshop; we will trace your frame digitally and fit brand new Crizal or Blue-Shield lenses.

30 - 45 Mins Save Money

30-Minute Express Lens Fitting

Our in-house computerized CNC laboratory stocks all popular single-vision prescription powers. Get your spectacles cut, beveled, polished and fitted in 30 minutes flat.

30 Mins Express Express Service

Frame Repair & Alignment

Has your frame become loose, tilted, or sat on accidentally? Our master optical technicians realign temple angles, replace bent hinges, and adjust bridge pads.

10 Mins Lifetime Free for Customers

Screw & Silicone Nose Pad Replacement
