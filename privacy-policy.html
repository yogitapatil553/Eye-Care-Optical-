Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the security of your optical consultation records are paramount to us.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Eye Care Optical ("we", "our", or "us"). This website is an informational portal designed to showcase optical eyewear, clinical services, store location, and to facilitate inquiries and appointment bookings via phone, email, and WhatsApp.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our website, book an eye examination, or contact our store, you may provide voluntary personal details including:

  • Your full name and contact telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Email address (if provided in contact forms).
  • Preferred appointment dates, time slots, and requested optical services.
  • Optional optical concerns or prescription details you disclose to help us prepare your consultation.

3. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your product inquiries and confirm appointment reservations.
  • Provide customer support, frame availability updates, and optical recommendations.
  • Ensure safety and clinical accuracy when you visit our Miraj showroom.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal contact information to any third-party marketing companies. Data is exclusively utilized by our internal optical clinic staff.

5. WhatsApp Communication

When you click WhatsApp enquiry links or submit an appointment request, your phone number and prefilled message are routed via WhatsApp Messenger under Meta's applicable end-to-end encryption privacy policies.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use standard session cookies or browser local storage to preserve your user preferences and provide smooth navigation. No sensitive financial information is ever processed or stored, as this website is strictly informational without online payment processing.

7. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions regarding this policy or wish to review your stored enquiry records, please visit us at Branch 1: Laxmi Market, Kisan Chowk | Branch 2: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Chowk, Miraj, or email us at info@eyecareoptical.com.
