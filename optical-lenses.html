Understanding Your Prescription Lens Options

Choosing the right lens geometry and coating determines how clearly and comfortably you see the world.

Single Vision Lenses

Features a uniform optical correction across the entire lens surface. Used to correct either distance (Myopia) or near vision (Reading / Hyperopia).

  • Ideal for under-40 individuals
  • Available in 30-min express fit
Most Recommended for 40+

FreeForm Progressive Lenses

Seamless continuous progression between distance, dashboard/computer, and near reading�with zero visible dividing line.

  • No image jump or dizziness
  • 3D digital pupillary customization
  • Essilor Varilux & Zeiss Precision

Photochromic (Transitions)

Intelligent lenses that remain crystal clear indoors and automatically transform into deep sunglass tint within seconds under sunlight.

  • 1 pair for both indoors & outdoors
  • Full UV400 sun blocking
