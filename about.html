About Eye Care Optical

15+ years of clinical optometry, bespoke eyewear styling, and unwavering commitment to healthy vision.

Our Legacy

Founded on Clinical Integrity & Exceptional Eyewear Craft

Established in 2010 in Miraj, Eye Care Optical was born out of a desire to bridge the gap between sterile medical eye clinics and superficial retail optical stores. Today, we proudly serve patients and eyewear enthusiasts across 2 prime branches: Laxmi Market (Kisan Chowk) and Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Chowk (Dasara Chowk).

We recognized that eyewear is both a medical necessity and an expression of personal character. Our optical showroom was thoughtfully designed to offer customers a relaxed, pressurized-free environment where qualified optometrists dedicate real time to examine your eyes, understand your screen habits, and help you select frames that complement your face architecture.

Over the past decade and a half, we have grown to care for over 50,000+ happy pairs of eyes�from toddlers getting their first flexible glasses to working professionals requiring blue-filter relief, and grandparents enjoying effortless reading with free-form progressive lenses.
